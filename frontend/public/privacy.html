Your privacy in Village

Effective September 30, 2026. Village is operated by the organization identified in the app’s store listing or by the website operator. Contact your church administrator for community access, directory concerns, or help identifying the operator.

Information you choose to provide

We store your name, account email, securely hashed password, church memberships, and the optional profile details you enter. We also store invitations, RSVPs and guest counts, practical needs, volunteer offers, meal-train details, meal commitments, comments, privacy choices, notification choices, and reports. A mobile device’s push token is stored only when you enable push notifications.

Who can see what

Private church features require a signed-in account and approved membership in that church. You choose whether you appear in the directory and whether members can see your contact or household details. These profile choices apply to all churches you join. Church administrators can see member account emails to manage access. Community posts and comments are visible to approved members of the church. Meal delivery addresses, contacts, and instructions are restricted to organizers, church administrators, and members who have claimed a meal date. We do not make private church data publicly searchable.

Why the information is used

Information is used to run the community, coordinate care, protect access, process reports, and deliver the categories of notifications you enable. Verification and password-reset emails are sent when requested. We do not sell your personal information or use advertising trackers. Email delivery and hosting providers process information needed to perform their services. If push is enabled, Firebase Cloud Messaging and Apple or Google device services deliver notifications; lock-screen text contains only a generic community update.

Devices and cookies

The website uses an essential HTTP-only session cookie. The native apps store the session credential in iOS Keychain or Android Keystore-backed secure storage. The app stores your selected church ID and pending invitation locally; it does not store private directory records for offline use. Camera access is used only when you choose to scan a church invitation QR code. Village does not upload camera images. QR scanning may use the platform and scanner libraries documented by its provider.

Your choices

In Settings you can edit your profile, change privacy and notifications, unblock members, export your own data, leave a church, or delete your account. Leaving releases upcoming RSVPs, volunteer places, and undelivered meal commitments. Deleting an account removes the profile, authored content, and account-associated records from the active database; a church owner must transfer ownership or delete the church first. Archived database backups expire under the operator’s published retention schedule and should not be restored without reapplying deletion requests. Community notifications are cleared from the active database after 90 days.

Children and sensitive details

Accounts are for adults. Parents may optionally describe their household, but should avoid children’s birthdays, school details, and other sensitive information. Share someone else’s need or meal-train details only with their permission. Anyone with access to information can potentially copy it; church members agree to respect the community’s privacy.

Contact and deletion requests

For support or an account-deletion request outside the app, email the operator’s support address listed in the app store. The operator will verify account ownership before processing a request. You can also delete your account directly within the app’s Settings.
