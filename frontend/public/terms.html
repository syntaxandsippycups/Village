A shared place. A little shared care.

Effective September 30, 2026. By creating an account you agree to these community terms and the privacy policy. Accounts are for adults who are at least 18.

Respect the people here

Use your real identity. Be kind. Do not post harassment, threats, hate, sexual exploitation, illegal material, spam, deceptive requests, or unauthorized commercial promotions. Do not copy, sell, redistribute, or use directory information outside the purposes people shared it for. Do not publish another person’s address, private contact information, children’s personal details, or sensitive situation without permission.

Invitations and offers are voluntary

Sharing a skill or resource is an invitation to connect, not a promise to accept every request. Coordinate logistics with the organizer. If you accept an invitation, volunteer place, or meal date and your plans change, cancel in the app so others can help. Obtain permission before arranging care or booking on another person’s behalf.

Church membership

Church access is controlled by its administrators. Do not share an access code or private link publicly without the church’s permission. A church may approve, decline, or remove membership and may moderate content. Accounts and church communities may be suspended when these terms are violated.

Care, judgment, and safety

Village coordinates connections; it does not screen volunteers or certify skills. Follow your church’s safeguarding procedures for childcare, rides, home visits, and vulnerable people. Verify food allergies with the recipient and organizer. Use your judgment before lending equipment or accepting services. Village is not an emergency service; use your local emergency number when urgent help is needed.

Report a concern

Use Report on a post, comment, or directory profile. Church administrators review reports and can remove harmful content or access. You can block another member from Settings or their directory entry. For account or operator concerns, contact the support address in the app-store listing or privacy page.

Your content and choices

You retain ownership of the content you share and permit the operator to store and display it to provide this service. You can edit your profile, manage your own posts and commitments, export your data, and delete your account. Features may change as the service improves. The operator will publish updates to these terms here.
